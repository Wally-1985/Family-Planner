# Development Notes

## Design direction

The UI is intentionally calm, minimal, and warm-neutral, inspired by the feel of modern AI workspaces like Claude: left navigation, soft cards, spacious layout, and switchable light/dark themes.

Do not copy Claude branding/assets. Keep Finances its own product.
